Summer STEM Robotics Camp for Kids Ages 4–14

Give your child a memorable summer with our hands-on STEM Robotics Summer Camp in Brentwood and Murfreesboro, TN. Designed for kids ages 4–14, this camp combines LEGO and VEX IQ robotics with exciting challenges that boost creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Robotics Summer Camp (AM)

8:00 AM – 11:45 AM | Ages 4–16

Summer Half-Day Robotics Camp (4 Hours / Day)
A fun, engaging and skill-building program designed to introduce children to robotics, coding, and logical thinking in a balanced, screen-free environment.

Daily Structure (Half-Day Program)
🕘 First 2 Hours – Robotics & Coding
  • Every student is assigned their own robot hardware and laptop
  • Students build and code robots daily
  • Projects are matched to the child’s skill level
  • Perfect for both absolute beginners and experienced students
🎲 Next 2 Hours – Indoor Games & Brain Play
  • Chess, board games, and strategy-based indoor activities
  • Encourages logical thinking
  • Builds focus and patience
  • Promotes social interaction and teamwork

In today’s era of iPads and video games, we intentionally promote screen-free board games to develop thinking skills and healthy habits.

🧠 Designed for All Skill Levels
  • No experience required for beginners
  • Advanced students receive more challenging projects
  • Students enrolling for multiple weeks experience gradual progression
  • Each week becomes more engaging and technically challenging
  • Parents may enroll for one week or multiple weeks based on interest
🤖 Friday Special: Fun Build & Battle Bots
  • Creative robot builds
  • Friendly robot challenges
  • Team competitions and fun engineering tasks
  • A favorite highlight for kids every week
🛡️ Safe, Structured & Supervised
  • All instructors are background verified
  • Instructors have been working with Robotix Institute for many months
  • Small group batches ensure personal attention
🍎 Snacks & What to Bring
  • Snacks are provided during the break
  • No need to bring laptops or robotics kits
  • Optional: Kids may bring extra snacks if they feel hungry
🚫 Screen-Free Learning Environment
  • No personal devices allowed
  • No TV time
  • Focused, distraction-free learning and play
✅ Why Parents Love This Program
  • Hands-on robotics every day
  • Healthy balance of learning and play
  • Screen-free environment
  • Progressive learning across weeks
  • Safe, instructor-led structure

Robotics Summer Camp (Full Day)

8:00 AM – 3:45 PM | Ages 4–16

Summer Full-Day Robotics Camp (8 Hours / Day)
Our Full-Day camp offers two complete learning sessions—one in the morning and one in the afternoon—designed for students who want deeper engagement and more hands-on time.

Daily Structure (Full-Day Program)
🌞 Morning Session (AM)
🤖 Robotics & Coding (2 Hours)
  • Each student is assigned their own robot hardware and laptop
  • Students build, program, and test robots daily
  • Projects are adapted to the child’s skill level (beginner to advanced)
🎲 Indoor Games & Strategy Play (2 Hours)
  • Chess, board games, and logic-based indoor activities
  • Encourages critical thinking, focus, and social interaction
  • Purposefully screen-free to reduce overexposure to devices
🌙 Afternoon Session (PM)

🔁 Same Structured Schedule as AM

  • Robotics & coding continues with new or advanced challenges
  • Indoor games and brain-building activities are repeated with:
    • Higher difficulty levels
    • Team challenges
    • Creative problem-solving tasks

The AM schedule is followed again in the PM, allowing students to learn more, build more, and explore deeper concepts.

🧠 Designed for All Skill Levels
  • Perfect for absolute beginners and advanced students
  • Advanced students receive progressively challenging projects
  • Multi-week enrollment leads to:
    • Gradual skill progression
    • More complex builds
    • Increased coding depth
  • Parents may enroll students for one week or multiple weeks based on interest
🤖 Friday Special: Fun Builds & Battle Bots
  • Creative engineering builds
  • Friendly Battle Bots challenges
  • Team-based robot competitions
  • One of the most exciting days of the week
🛡️ Safe, Structured & Supervised
  • All instructors are background verified
  • Instructors have been working with Robotix Institute for many months
  • Small group sizes ensure individual attention
🍎 Lunch, Snacks & What to Bring
  • Snacks are provided during breaks
  • Students bring their own lunch
  • No need to bring laptops or robotics hardware
  • Optional: extra snacks if needed
🚫 Screen-Free Learning Environment
  • No personal devices allowed
  • No TV or recreational screen time
  • Focused, distraction-free learning and creativity
✅ Why Choose the Full-Day Camp?
  • Two full robotics sessions daily
  • Maximum hands-on learning time
  • Balanced learning and play
  • Screen-free, structured environment
  • Progressive multi-week curriculum

VEX Boot Camp

8:30 AM – 3:30 PM

VEX Robotics Boot Camp – Full-Day Engineering & Coding Program
The VEX Robotics Boot Camp is an intensive, full-day robotics program designed for students who want to go beyond basic robotics and build strong engineering, programming, and problem-solving skills.

This boot camp is 100% robotics focused—from the start of the day until the end—with no games or distractions. Students spend the entire day designing, building, coding, testing, and improving robots using the VEX platform.

🛠️ What Students Learn
Engineering & Design
  • Robot chassis design and structural stability
  • Gear ratios, torque vs speed, and drivetrain optimization
  • Mechanisms such as arms, claws, lifts, and intakes
  • Iterative engineering process: build → test → improve
Programming & Coding
  • Coding robots using VEX programming environments
  • Motor control, sensors, and autonomous movement
  • Logic, loops, and conditional statements
  • Introduction to competition-style coding strategies
Problem Solving & Teamwork
  • Breaking complex problems into smaller steps
  • Debugging mechanical and coding issues
  • Collaboration and communication within teams
  • Developing an engineering mindset and critical thinking
⏱️ Full-Day Robotics Structure
  • Entire day dedicated to robotics, engineering, and coding
  • Students work on progressively challenging projects
  • Activities are adjusted based on experience level
  • Ideal for students preparing for:
    • VEX competitions
    • Advanced robotics pathways
    • STEM-focused academic programs
🧠 Designed for Beginners & Advanced Students
  • Beginners receive structured guidance and foundational skills
  • Advanced students are challenged with:
    • Complex builds
    • Advanced coding logic
    • Competition-style tasks
  • Multi-week enrollment allows for deep skill progression
🛡️ Safe, Focused & Instructor-Led
  • Small group instruction for personalized learning
  • Background-verified instructors
  • Structured, distraction-free learning environment
  • No personal devices or entertainment screens
✅ Why Choose the VEX Boot Camp?
  • 100% robotics immersion
  • Strong engineering and coding foundation
  • Real-world problem-solving skills
  • Ideal for competition-ready students
  • Builds confidence and technical depth

LEGO Boot Camp

9:00 AM – 3:45 PM | Ages 8–10

LEGO Robotics Boot Camp – Full-Day Engineering & Coding Program
The LEGO Robotics Boot Camp is a full-day, immersive STEM program designed to introduce students to engineering, robotics, and coding through hands-on LEGO-based projects.

This program is 100% robotics focused—from the beginning of the day until the end—allowing students to spend the entire day building, coding, testing, and improving robots using LEGO robotics platforms.

🛠️ What Students Learn
Engineering & Design
  • Building stable robot bases using LEGO components
  • Understanding gears, motion, and mechanical movement
  • Designing mechanisms such as grabbers, arms, and movers
  • Learning the engineering process: plan → build → test → improve
Programming & Coding
  • Coding robots using beginner-friendly LEGO programming environments
  • Understanding sequences, loops, and conditional logic
  • Using sensors for movement, detection, and decision-making
  • Developing logical thinking and computational skills
Problem Solving & Teamwork
  • Breaking problems into simple steps
  • Debugging builds and code
  • Collaborating with peers on design challenges
  • Building confidence and creativity through hands-on learning
⏱️ Full-Day Robotics Structure
  • Entire day dedicated to LEGO robotics and coding
  • Students work on progressively challenging builds and programs
  • Instruction is differentiated based on age and experience
  • Ideal for students beginning their journey in robotics and STEM
🧠 Designed for Beginners & Developing Learners
  • No prior robotics or coding experience required
  • Projects are adapted for different age groups and skill levels

About RobotiX Summer Camps

What Is STEM RobotiX Summer Camp?

RobotiX Summer Camps are week-long, hands-on robotics experiences designed for kids in grades K–8. Each day, students build and program robots using LEGO® and VEX IQ while learning teamwork, problem-solving, and creativity.

What your Child Will Learn?

  • Kids work with real robots, sensors, and motors—not just screens.
  • Each day combines guided lessons, challenges, and friendly competitions.
  • Small group sizes and trained instructors ensure every child is supported.
