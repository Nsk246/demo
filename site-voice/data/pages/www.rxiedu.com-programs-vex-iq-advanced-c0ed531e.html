About VEX IQ Advanced

What Is VEX IQ Advanced?

Introduction to advanced concepts of robotics using VEX IQ Kits, focusing on Critical-thinking and problem-solving skills.

What your Child Will Learn?

  • Advanced programming with VEXcode
  • Competition-ready robot design
  • Strategic thinking
  • Engineering notebook documentation

Program Schedule

How Our VEX IQ Advanced Works

A consistent weekly routine helps young children feel comfortable, while the activities become slightly more challenging over time.

Week 01 : Competition Analysis

Analyzing the current VEX IQ challenge.

Week 02 : Design & Prototyping

Building prototypes for specific tasks.

Week 03 : Autonomous Programming

Coding the robot to move on its own.

Week 04 : Driver Control Skills

Practicing driving skills for matches.

Why Parents Love Our Program

Designed specifically for younger children, this program balances play and learning in a calm, supportive setting.

Builds confidence
Kids feel proud when their robot moves or reacts the way they designed.
Encourages creativity
Children are free to experiment, redesign, and personalize their builds.
Develops early STEM skills
Introduces core ideas like cause-and-effect, patterns, and sequences.
Hands-on time
Focus is on building and exploring with real bricks and parts, not just screens.
