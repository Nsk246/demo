Seasonal Robotics Camps for Kids Ages 4–14

Explore Our Camps

Make every school break exciting with our STEM-focused Robotics Camps in Brentwood and Murfreesboro, TN. From Summer, Fall, Winter, to Spring, children explore LEGO and VEX IQ robotics while building creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills. At RobotiX Institute, our year-round Robotics Camps for kids ages 4–14 give students the chance to learn, build, and create while having fun during every school break. Offered at our Brentwood and Murfreesboro, TN locations, our seasonal STEM camps — Summer Camp (June–July), Fall Camp (October), Winter Camp (December), and Spring Camp (March) — combine hands-on robotics with engaging challenges using LEGO Robotics and VEX IQ Robotics. Each camp is designed to spark curiosity, encourage creativity, and build essential skills like problem-solving, teamwork, and critical thinking.
